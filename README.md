# Anime Champions X: release date and the road to it

**Anime Champions X** is the next Roblox game from daireb, the developer of Anime Champions Simulator. It releases on **26 November 2026**; the developer has given a day and no hour.

This repository holds the dated list of what the developer has posted, as data, and a calendar file for release day. It is the data behind the [Anime Champions X Wiki](https://animechampionsx.wiki/), an unofficial fan wiki.

- [Release date and countdown](https://animechampionsx.wiki/release-date)
- [Codes](https://animechampionsx.wiki/codes): none exist before release
- [Official Discord](https://animechampionsx.wiki/discord) · [Roblox page](https://animechampionsx.wiki/roblox) · [Trailer](https://animechampionsx.wiki/trailer) · [News](https://animechampionsx.wiki/news)

Files: [`timeline.json`](timeline.json) (every post, oldest first) and [`anime-champions-x-release.ics`](anime-champions-x-release.ics) (add release day to a calendar).

## Timeline

- **24 June 2026** · Sneak peeks. The first post in the sneak-peeks channel: two attachments and no caption.
- **10 July 2026** · Sneak peeks. A short clip of a burning town, seen over a character's shoulder. No caption.
- **16 September 2026** · Announcements. daireb returns after two months: “ok gang it's me, your boy daireb. Sorry for the silence, we've been busy in the kitchen”, then “it's time for some new sneaks”.
- **18 September 2026** · Sneak peeks. “Ok fine… I didn't even want to show you this sneak anyways…”, with a clip.
- **5 October 2026** · Sneak peeks. “How it feels to pull up with a new anime game in 2026...” The clip opens on painted clouds, then lands in a market street where characters stand in a cyan glow.
- **8 October 2026** · Sneak peeks. “do you guys like our sound design”. A fight in a harbour town: fireballs and a long orange slash across the square.
- **20 October 2026** · Sneak peeks. “Can't wait to show you guys what we're cooking up! Big news soon”.
- **23 October 2026** · Sneak peeks. “Are you ready?” A crimson-armoured figure in a hall of blue stained glass, ending on a “coming soon” card. The same day daireb said the server's chat channels would open the following week.
- **29 October 2026** · Sneak peeks. A swordfighter in a walled courtyard with torii gates, trading purple and blue slashes. In announcements: “I wonder if anything will happen TOMORROW... Probably not tho”.
- **30 October 2026** · Announcements. The Discord's chat channels open: “chats are now open”.
- **5 November 2026** · Announcements. A poll, “Release date + trailer tomorrow?”, closes with 96% voting yes.
- **6 November 2026** · Announcements. The release date trailer premieres on YouTube. Afterwards: “Mark the date: Anime Champions X releases on the 26th of November. We'll share gameplay and more details soon :)”. The server's tag changes from ??? to ACX.
- **7 November 2026** · Server news. A content-creator application form opens, a 10% server XP boost is added for members wearing the ACX tag, and English and Portuguese moderator applications open. They closed on 11 November.
- **8 November 2026** · Announcements. “GAMEPLAY SNEAKS THIS WEEK”.
- **10 November 2026** · Sneak peeks. A fly-over of an open map: teal sky, blue grass, giant mushrooms, white pod houses and a view from high above. daireb added: “(runs higher FPS than ACS by the way)”.
- **11 November 2026** · Sneak peeks. “W or L VFX?” An attack that floods a stone courtyard with black ink and briefly turns the whole screen black and white.
